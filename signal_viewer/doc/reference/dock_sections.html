Sections dock

Lists the sections of the active channel and holds the section commands. Shown on the Graph page; View > Sections brings it back when closed. How-to: Working with sections.

Command bar

ActionEnabled whenEffect
New SectionThe base section is activeShows the region selector on the plot and the Confirm / Cancel buttons.
Lock SectionAn unlocked user section is activeComputes the final result of the section in the background and locks it. Needs at least three peaks.
Unlock SectionA locked user section is activeMakes the section editable again; its result stays until the next change.
Remove SectionAn unlocked user section is activeDeletes the section with its peaks and results. Cannot be undone. Later sections are renumbered.
Show Section SummaryAlwaysOpens a dialog with name, size, sampling rate, bounds, peak count and the processing history of the active section.
Section OverviewThe base section is activeShows the sections as coloured regions on the plot. Switched on automatically when the base section becomes active.

Confirm and Cancel

Visible only while a new section is being selected. Confirm creates the section from the region selector's range and makes it active; Cancel drops the region.

Section list

  • Active Section above the list names the section being edited.
  • Each entry shows the section's name, Section 000 (CHANNEL), and a lock icon: closed for locked sections (and the base section), open for editable ones.
  • Click an entry to make it active. Right-click for a context menu with Remove Section and Show Section Summary.
  • Hover an entry for a summary tooltip: size, sampling rate, bounds, peak count and processing history.
